Mariana UFO incident

Filmed by local baseball coach, Nick Mariana in Great Falls, Montana.

He and his secretary Virginia Raunig were inspecting the empty Legion Stadium baseball field before a game. A bright flash caught Mariana's eye and he reportedly saw two bright silvery objects, rotating while flying over Great Falls at a speed he estimated to be two hundred to four hundred miles per hour. He believed that they were roughly fifty feet wide and one hundred and fifty feet apart.
